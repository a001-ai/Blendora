Resin Care & Longevity Guide

Every Blendora Hub piece is hand-poured with premium UV-stabilized jewelry grade resin. Follow these simple tips to keep your treasures pristine for years.

Avoid Direct Sun Storage

While our resin contains advanced UV inhibitors, prolonged direct sun exposure on windowsills can soften cured resin over time. Store in a shaded jewelry box.

Keep Away from Harsh Chemicals

Apply your lotions, perfumes, and hairsprays before putting on your resin jewelry. Never expose resin to acetone, alcohol rubs, or chlorine pools.

Gentle Microfiber Cleaning

Wipe fingerprints or dust away with a soft, dry microfiber cloth. For stubborn residue, use lukewarm water and a drop of mild dish soap, then dry thoroughly.

Scratch-Free Storage

Resin is durable and impact resistant, but sharp metal objects like loose house keys can cause micro-scratches. Keep in individual pouches when traveling.
